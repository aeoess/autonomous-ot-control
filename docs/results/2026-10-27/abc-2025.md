# A/B/C With Faults, 2025

Seeds 177, 178, 179. Strategies: P0, P3raw, P3. Scope: Synthetic, uncalibrated building-services twin (three-zone-hvac-seasonal-v2) at fictitious sites in each Norwegian price area. Verified 2025 day-ahead prices per area and local ERA5 weather. Cost = spot + a declared reference grid tariff (ASSUMPTION; taxes excluded). Strategies applied directly (offline); not a real-building saving. Seeded sensor faults (frozen and drifted zone sensors) in every strategy.
